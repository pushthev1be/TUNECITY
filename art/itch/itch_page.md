## Page description

**12 stylised low-poly cars, split and ready to drive.**

These are the cars from **TuneCity**, my pixel-art car-tuning game, rebuilt as clean 3D models. From a 90s hot hatch to a lifted desert pickup, every car comes apart the way a game needs it to: the hood opens on its real hinge, all four wheels roll about their hubs, and there's an engine sitting in the bay.

### Built for games
- **11 objects per car:** body · hood (engine lid on the two mid/rear-engine cars) · engine · 4 wheels · 4 brake calipers
- **Real pivots:** the hood's origin sits on its hinge (rotate X to open); each wheel's origin sits at its hub (rotate X to roll); calipers stay put
- **Real-world scale:** metres, about 4–5 m long, ground at zero
- **Light:** about 11–13k triangles per car
- **Zero setup:** flat-shaded solid colours, one simple material per colour, no textures to wire up

### What's in the download
- **GLB** for Godot, three.js, Babylon and Unity (glTFast)
- **FBX** for Unity and Unreal (−Z forward, Y up, scale applied)
- **OBJ + MTL** for anything else
- **.blend** source files (Blender 3.6+)
- A **preview render** of every car, plus the full lineup
- **Bonus:** each car as a 16-angle **pixel-art turntable sprite strip**, the same art the game uses

### Notes
These are original, stylised designs inspired by car types. No manufacturer names, logos or badges are used.
